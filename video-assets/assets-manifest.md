## Missing or limited (needs a decision from you)

1. **No logo or wordmark file.** The only mark is the extension icon. "WebSeal" in the video would be typeset text in the system font.
2. **The icon's rounded top is cut off at the image edge.** That is how `icons.mjs` draws it, at every size including the shipped 16–128 px. I did not change it. Fixing it means editing the icon design, so tell me if you want that.
3. **No vector (SVG) icon.** The 512/1024 PNGs come from the same per-pixel drawing code, so they're sharp at those sizes.
4. **No diagram assets.** The "How it works" 3-block diagram has to be made in the video step.
5. **MetaMask screenshots: done** (checked 2026-11-06). The automated set was re-taken in dark mode to match them (`--dark`).
   - `mm-02`: MetaMask To = your address.
   - `mm-03`: red banner, no MetaMask.
   - `mm-04`: MetaMask To = `0xBAd0…0Bad` while the page shows the address you typed.
   - `mm-01`: connect prompt, spare.
   - They show the browser tab bar (including a WhatsApp tab), your profile icon, and account `0x264F…1018E` / `smxreds.eth` with its balance. **Crop to the page and MetaMask panel** in the video. Decide whether the account address may be shown.
   - The MetaMask shots send 0.01 ETH, the automated ones 0.0001: captions should not quote an amount.
6. **No brand guide, font files, music or voice-over.** The voice-over script is in the storyboard; recording it is yours.
7. **Every screenshot shows `localhost:4174`.** If the site is deployed (AWS) before the video, re-run `node scripts/screenshots.mjs --out video-assets/screenshots/wagmi-flow --scale 2`.
8. **wagmi's own error text on the blocked send is generic** ("The requested method and/or account has not been authorized by the user"). The red banner is what explains the block, which is why `16-…-with-banner` is the main Blocked shot.
